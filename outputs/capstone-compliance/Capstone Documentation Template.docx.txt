P232 [Normal] II. DETAILED CONTENT DESCRIPTION
P233 [Normal] Chapter 1: The "Why"
P234 [Normal] Project Context: Describe the current situation of the organization. What is the "pain point"?
P235 [Normal] Objectives: The General Objective is the "what" (To develop a system...). Specific Objectives are the "how" (To design, To implement, To test).
P236 [Normal] Scope & Delimitation: Be very specific. If you aren't doing SMS notifications, put it in Delimitation so the panel won't ask for it.
P237 [Normal] Chapter 2: The "Foundation"
P238 [Normal] RRL/RRS: Don't just copy-paste. Explain how each study relates to your project.
P239 [Normal] Synthesis: Summarize the gap. "Existing systems lack X, which our system provides."
P240 [Normal] Chapter 3: The "Tech Stack"
P241 [Normal] Hardware/Software Specs: List the minimum and recommended requirements to run your app.
P242 [Normal] Chapter 4: The "How" (Most Important for IT)
P243 [Normal] SDLC: Most students use Agile or Iterative. Explain your sprints/phases.
P244 [Normal] Diagrams: These must be technically accurate. Your ERD must match your actual database tables.
P245 [Normal] Evaluation Method: Explain how you used the ISO/IEC 25010 standard to let users rate your software.
P246 [Normal] Chapter 5: The "Output"
P247 [Normal] Screenshots: Include clear screenshots of your best features.
P248 [Normal] Data Tables: Present the mean scores of your surveys (Usability, Security, Reliability).
P249 [Normal] Chapter 6: The "Wrap-up"
P250 [Normal] Conclusion: Must directly answer your Objectives in Chapter 1. If you had 5 objectives, you should have 5 corresponding conclusions.
