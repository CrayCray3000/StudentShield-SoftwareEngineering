Figure 2. Context Diagram of StudentShield Vent Screening and Bullying Reporting


COPY — replacement explanation after the redraw

Figure 2 shows the boundary of the StudentShield web application for vent screening and bullying reporting. Authenticated students submit vents and incident reports, with optional incident evidence. The application sends current vent text, a student identifier, and prior risk labels and timestamps from a 14-day window to the external machine learning service. Screening results support staff review. High-risk vents trigger counselor notification attempts, while Critical vents may also trigger parent notifications subject to the parent-hiding option and applicable delivery conditions. Bullying intake alerts counselors; staff verification precedes incident notifications to resolved parent contacts. A separate false-report procedure may notify the reporter’s parent after a second rejected report. This partial context view excludes the online meeting workflow.
