Use one visual system

Use Outfit for headings and Inter for text, fields, and buttons. Set important body text around 16 px and supporting instructions around 14 px. Use 44–48 px controls, 16–24 px content padding, modest corner rounding, and subtle borders. Keep gold as a small school accent.
