# DFD Level 2 (Sub-Process Decomposition) — Explanation

The DFD Level 2 diagrams explain exactly what happens to a student's information from the moment they submit it until it is safely stored or flagged for help. This level focuses on the two most sensitive parts of StudentShield: sharing personal feelings and reporting incidents of bullying.

When a student uses the venting feature to share their thoughts, the system begins by capturing their words and current mood. This information is immediately sent to an artificial intelligence service that looks for signs of high emotional distress or safety risks. Once the analysis is complete, the system secures the data by scrambling the text through encryption before saving it. This ensures that personal thoughts remain private and protected. Finally, the system compares the risk level found by the AI against safety thresholds. If the student appears to be in danger, a trigger is sent to the notification system to alert a counselor immediately.

The process for reporting bullying follows a similarly careful path to ensure every case is handled correctly. It starts by gathering the specific details of the incident along with any supporting photos or videos. Before the report is finalized, the system runs a check to make sure the evidence files meet the necessary size and format requirements. After this validation, the entire report is securely locked away in the database. To make sure no report goes unnoticed, the system then places the case into a verification queue, which automatically updates the counselor's dashboard so they can begin their review as soon as possible.
