 4.0  Scope  and  Limitations  of  the  Study:   Scope:   ●  The  study  focuses  on  designing  and  developing  the  StudentShield  web  platform  using  PHP  CodeIgniter,  including  venting  forms,  bullying  trackers,  ML  sentiment  analysis  integration,  and  Twilio  SMS  automation.  ●  It  will  cover  full  implementation  for  SVBSM  Junior  High  School  students  with  admin  oversight  features.  ●  Pilot  testing  will  validate  detection  accuracy  and  parent  notification  impact  on  behavior.   Limitations:   ●  The  system  targets  only  SVBSM  Junior  High  School   initially,  with  testing  in  a  controlled  school  environment  before  broader  rollout.  ●  ML  models  will  use  basic  sentiment  classification  and  pattern  detection  rather  than  advanced  deep  learning.  SMS  functionality  depends  on  Twilio  service  availability  and  parent  phone  number  accuracy.  ●  The  study  excludes  long-term  psychological  impact  evaluation  and  physical  hardware  integration,  focusing  solely  on  software  functionality.   5.0  Target  Beneficiaries:   1.  Junior  High  School  Students  at  SVBSM:  StudentShield  offers  a  safe  space  to  anonymously  vent  frustrations  and  depression  symptoms,  reducing  isolation  while  tracking  bullying  encounters  to  protect  victims  and  encourage  better  behavior  in  perpetrators.  2.  Parents/Guardians:  Receive  automated  SMS  alerts  about  high-risk  incidents  (phrased  as  wellness  checks),  enabling  immediate  home  support  while  simultaneously  alerting  counselors  for  professional  follow-up.  3.  School  Counselors:  Gain  admin  dashboards  with  ML-driven  insights  on  behavioral  trends,  high-risk  students,  and  incident  patterns  for  proactive  interventions  and  reduced  classroom  disruptions.  4.  SVBSM  Administration:  Benefits  from  a  data-backed  system  that  lowers  bullying/depression  rates,  improves  overall  student  well-being,  and  demonstrates  commitment  to  modern  mental  health  solutions.   6.0  References:   ●  McCloud,  T.,  Jones,  R.,  Lewis,  G.,  Bell,  V.,  &  Tsakanikos,  E.
