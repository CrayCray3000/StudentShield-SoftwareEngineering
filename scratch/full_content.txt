ABSTRACT

This capstone project presents the development of BOSS: Blue Aura's Operational Smart System for Booking, Analytics, and Payroll, a web-based platform designed to streamline the daily operations of Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise Resort. The system addresses the limitations of manual processes by automating reservation handling, generating data-driven analytics, and accurately computing employee payroll. To enhance user interaction, the system integrates a built-in chatbot that responds to common client inquiries and guides users throughout the platform, with an option to connect with a live agent when additional support is needed.

The system was developed with a modular structure comprising the Booking Module, Analytics Module, and Payroll Module. The Booking Module simplifies reservation creation and management, while the Analytics Module provides visual insights on business performance through dashboards and reports. The Payroll Module automates salary computation based on attendance and work hours, reducing manual workload and human error. Access control features ensure that only authorized staff and administrators can view sensitive information and perform administrative operations.

To determine the system's effectiveness, the proponents conducted an evaluation using ISO-based criteria with two groups of respondents: users and IT experts. Results showed high levels of acceptance, with mean scores ranging from Good to Excellent for end users and Excellent for IT experts, indicating that the system meets key standards for functionality, reliability, usability, and performance.

Overall, the BOSS system significantly improves operational efficiency, enhances staff productivity, and provides a more seamless experience for both clients and administrators. The implementation of this system positions Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise for improved service delivery and future organizational growth.

CHAPTER I
Introduction

In today's fast-paced, technology-driven world, efficiency and convenience are essential to customer service, especially in the hospitality industry. However, many small businesses continue to rely on traditional, manual processes that no longer meet the expectations of modern travelers. Most guests now prefer a seamless, digital booking experience, and resorts that fail to adapt risk losing both customer trust and potential revenue.

Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise, a privately managed resort renowned for its serene atmosphere and scenic environment, exemplifies this challenge. The resort currently uses manual systems for reservations, payroll, and other tasks, which are susceptible to human error and inefficiencies. These include frequent double bookings, delayed response times to customer inquiries, and inaccuracies in salary computations and sales documentation. Furthermore, the lack of a data-driven analytics system limits management's ability to make timely, informed decisions, hindering overall efficiency and long-term growth.

This study aims to address these challenges by designing an integrated Resort Management System that automates key operational areas, including reservation management, customer service, payroll, and analytics. The primary goal is to enhance the resort's overall operations through digital transformation, providing both staff and guests with a more streamlined, reliable, and responsive service infrastructure. Specifically, the system will include an online reservation platform with real-time booking confirmations, a chatbot to handle guest inquiries, an analytics dashboard for monitoring trends and performance, and modules for automated payroll.

Project Context
Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise, a private resort in North Caloocan City, faces several operational challenges that hinder its efficiency and growth. The resort relies heavily on manual processes to manage key business functions, including reservations, payroll, and sales. The resort's manual reservation system increases the risk of double bookings, scheduling conflicts, and delayed customer responses, often taking more than an hour. These delays and errors result in poor customer satisfaction and lost bookings, which affect the resort's reputation and revenue.

To address the challenges, this project proposes the development of a new system to ensure the business runs smoothly. The developed system will allow guests to book online, receive instant confirmation, and easily make changes to their reservations. The system will include a chatbot to quickly answer the most common questions, benefiting customers who won't have to wait long for a response. Another feature the system will have is helping the resort staff by tracking guest bookings, analytics trends, and payments. The payroll system will also simplify how staff salaries and resort agents' commissions are calculated, ensuring the process is accurate and faster.

The improvements that the system will provide aim to reduce errors, save time, and improve the experience for both staff and guests. This will help Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise become more organized, efficient, and customer-friendly, supporting its long-term success and growth.

Objective of the Study
General Objective of the Study
To develop and implement a comprehensive Resort Management System for Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise that enhances operational efficiency by integrating automation, analytics, and payroll management tools. This system aims to streamline reservations, improve customer service, optimize financial processes, and provide real-time insights to support effective decision-making.

Specific Objectives of the Study
A. Reservation and Customer Service

a. Implement an online reservation system with instant booking confirmation to minimize scheduling conflicts and double bookings.
b. Allow customers to reschedule and transfer booking contracts seamlessly within the system, ensuring a hassle-free experience without lengthy manual approval processes.
c. Integrate a chatbot to automate responses to frequently asked questions, reducing response time and providing instant customer support.
d. Ensure that complex customer inquiries are efficiently escalated to human staff for personalized assistance.

B. Analytics and Marketing

a. Develop an analytics module to track and analyze booking trends, occupancy rates, and promotional effectiveness.
b. Provide real-time insights into customer engagement, sales performance, and staff responsiveness to aid in operational decision-making.
c. Convert customer inquiries into measurable booking metrics to enhance marketing evaluation and strategic planning.

C. Payroll Management System

a. Automate payroll processing, including salary computation, agent commissions, and deductions.
b. Implement an online salary disbursement system supporting GCash and bank transfers for seamless transactions.
c. Automate the tracking and calculation of agent commissions and staff salaries to eliminate manual processes, ensuring accurate and timely payments while enhancing efficiency and reducing errors.

This approach ensures that the Resort Management System effectively enhances efficiency, customer experience, and financial management while supporting data-driven business operations.

Significance of the Study
The significance of this study lies in its ability to enhance the overall operations of Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise by implementing a Resort Management System that automates essential operational processes. Currently, the resort relies on a manual system for handling customer service, booking, and reservation management. This manual approach presents several challenges, including time-consuming processes, a high volume of human errors, and difficulties in efficiently tracking reservations. These issues can lead to booking conflicts, delays in customer service, and inefficiencies in managing guest inquiries, ultimately affecting both the customer experience and the resort's operational productivity.

By developing and integrating a streamlined, automated, and user-friendly digital platform, this study aims to optimize these processes, ensuring a more efficient, accurate, and convenient system for both the management team and guests. The proposed system will not only improve booking accuracy and customer satisfaction but will also allow resort staff to focus more on enhancing guest services rather than being burdened by repetitive administrative tasks. Additionally, this will also provide real-time data tracking, better resource allocation, and a structured approach to managing reservations, ultimately contributing to the long-term growth and sustainability of Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise.

Scope and Delimitation of the Study
Scope
This study focuses on the design, development, and implementation of a Resort Management System specifically for Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise Resort. The system aims to automate and improve key operational areas such as:

1.) Reservation and Customer Service Features:
Online Booking: Customers can make real-time reservations through the system. The system will automatically check availability and confirm bookings instantly, helping avoid double bookings or scheduling conflicts.
Booking Dashboard: Customers will have access to a personal dashboard where they can reschedule or transfer bookings without requiring manual approval from staff, and only once.
Chatbot Integration: The chatbot will handle the frequently asked questions (FAQs) and provide quick, automated responses. For concerns that require more detailed or personal attention, the system will automatically forward the inquiry to a human staff member who can assist the guest directly.

2.) Analytics and Marketing Features:
Analytics Dashboard: The system will include an analytics dashboard that collects and presents data on bookings, occupancy rates, customer inquiries, and seasonal trends. Management can use this data to identify the most profitable months, determine which promotions are most effective, and assess how responsive the staff is to customer queries.
Analytics Algorithm: The analytics algorithm will be exclusively designed to process and analyze historical data from Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise resort. This will include, but is not limited to, booking history, transaction records, and agent commission reports.

3.) Payroll Management System Feature:
Payroll System: The system will handle payroll automation, including the automatic computation of staff salaries, deductions, and agent commissions. This minimizes errors and saves time compared to manual payroll processing.
Payout Options: Employees and agents will receive payments through integrated channels such as GCash or bank transfers, enabling faster, more convenient transactions.
Income and Expense Tracking: The system will track and summarize all income from bookings and corkages, as well as expenses, ensuring accurate financial records.
Financial Reporting: The system will generate reports showing the resort's capital, profit, and loss, providing management with a clear picture of its financial health. These insights are essential for planning budgets and setting financial goals.

4.) User Access and Role Management:
Role-Based Access: The Resort Management System will implement role-based access, granting different users varying levels of access based on their roles within the resort.
User-Specific Controls: This ensures that each user only sees the features and information relevant to their responsibilities, helping keep the system secure, organized, and user-friendly.

Limitations
The Resort Management System for Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise Resort is designed specifically for the resort and cannot be used by other resorts.

It requires a stable internet connection to function, as offline access is not supported.
The system will not document payments made outside it, and external financial audits are not supported. Staff will need training to use the system's different user roles effectively.
Basic security features are in place; further upgrades may be needed for stronger data protection, and regular updates will be necessary to maintain security and functionality.
The system does not track guests' real-time presence or activities during their stay, and the chatbot will only provide pre-programmed responses, with an option to escalate complex inquiries to a human agent.
The analytics module will not track bookings or interactions through external platforms like Facebook, and the system will not integrate with third-party booking sites such as Airbnb, Agoda, or Booking.com.
The payment method will not rely on third-party payment APIs, which may introduce constraints related to integration, transaction processing, and external service availability.

Payroll System
Murla et al. (2020) conducted an assessment of school-based payroll systems across fourteen schools in the Department of Education in Nueva Ecija, focusing on large institutions. The study, which used a descriptive research design, evaluated payroll systems in terms of accuracy, safety & security, efficiency, usability, and timeliness. The findings revealed that schools primarily used Microsoft Excel for payroll calculations. However, areas such as safety & security, efficiency, and timeliness were identified as needing improvement. The major issue highlighted was the significant time required to prepare payroll, pointing to the need for technological advancements to create a more accurate, secure, and efficient system. This study suggests that automated systems could improve the overall payroll process in schools, thus enhancing efficiency and reducing errors.

Rusly et al. (2020) conducted a bibliometric analysis of 170 publications from 1937 to 2019, exploring the critical role of payroll systems in ensuring accurate compensation and regulatory compliance. The study found increasing scholarly interest in payroll systems, particularly in recent years. It noted that systems like Sage, MYOB, and MySQL have significantly improved payroll accuracy and efficiency in Malaysia. The study advocates for the continued development of automated payroll systems to better manage large-scale operations and suggests that further research is needed to support technological advancements in this area.

Onuotu and Amaewhule (2024) explored the impact of automated accounting systems on payroll preparation among SMEs in Port Harcourt. Their survey of 400 SMEs found that tools such as Square Payroll and QuickBooks improved payroll efficiency by reducing errors and processing time. Technical Background
The proposed BOSS: Resort Management and Reservation System will be developed using a combination of modern web technologies to ensure responsiveness, scalability, and functionality suited to the resort's operational needs. The system will be built using PHP as the backend language and MySQL as the database management system to handle and store reservation data, employee records, and financial transactions. For the frontend, HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript will be used to structure and design the user interface, while Bootstrap 5 will ensure the layout is responsive and mobile-friendly. AJAX or the Fetch API will be used to enable asynchronous interactions, such as checking room availability without page reloads. To generate visual reports and analytics for admin users, Chart.js or APIs will be integrated into the dashboard module. PHPMailer will be used to send automated booking confirmations via email and text message, as some people do not check their email. Additionally, chatbot functionality will be implemented via a third-party API, such as Tidio or Dialogflow, to handle real-time customer inquiries. For local development and testing, XAMPP will be used as the server environment. The system will be deployed on a web hosting platform such as Hostinger or cPanel to ensure 24/7 online accessibility. These technologies will work together to build a complete, efficient, and user-friendly resort management platform.

Definition of Terms
Analytics Dashboard – A real-time visual interface that displays the key performance indicators (KPIs) for a website, often in the form of interactive charts. This can show website traffic, booking trends, and finance tracking to help the business make better decisions.

API (Application Programming Interface) – A set of protocols, routines, and tools that allow different software applications to communicate and interact with each other, enabling data exchange and functionality integration between systems.

Bootstrap – An open-source front-end framework that provides pre-designed templates, components, and tools for building responsive and mobile-first websites. It simplifies website design by providing ready-to-use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript components.

Chatbot – An automated assistant in the system that can reply to common questions from guests right away. If it gets a question it can't handle, it sends it to a real staff member for help.

Design of Software, System, Product or Process
Software Architecture
The Blue Aura Operational Smart System (BOSS) was designed using a multi-layered software architecture to ensure a modular, scalable, and efficient resort management solution. The system follows a standard web-based client-server model, utilizing a combination of frontend technologies, backend logic, and a centralized database to handle core operations such as booking, payroll, and analytics.

Layered Architecture Overview

1. Frontend (User Interface Layer):
The user interface is built using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. This layer is responsible for providing a responsive and interactive experience for guests, staff, and administrators. It handles user inputs through web forms and displays real-time data from the backend.

2. Backend (Logic Layer):
The core logic of BOSS is implemented using PHP. This layer manages the processing of business rules, such as calculating payroll, processing room availability, and managing user roles. It communicates between the frontend and the database, ensuring that data is processed securely and accurately.

3. Data Layer (Storage Layer):
A MySQL database serves as the central repository for all system data. This includes guest information, booking records, employee payroll data, and content for the integrated chatbot. The database is structured to support relational data management and efficient query retrieval.

4. Integration Layer:
BOSS incorporates several external integrations to enhance functionality:
PHPMailer: For sending automated email notifications to guests regarding their bookings and status.
Tidio/Dialogflow: Used for the implementation of the intelligent chatbot, which handles customer inquiries.
Chart.js: Utilized within the admin dashboard to visualize booking and financial data through interactive charts.

Modules and User Interaction
The system is compartmentalized into specific modules, each catering to different user roles:
Guest Module: Allows users to view resort information, check room availability, and submit booking requests.
Admin Module: Provides administrators with a comprehensive dashboard to manage reservations, monitor system performance, and access financial analytics.
Staff Module: Enables resort employees to view assigned tasks, update work statuses, and access relevant operational data.
Payroll Module: Specifically designed for HR and management to automate salary calculations based on commission, attendance, and tax deductions.

This architectural design ensures that BOSS is not only robust and secure but also flexible enough to accommodate future updates and additional features as the resort's operational needs evolve.

Architectural Diagram
[Figure 2: Software Architecture Diagram]

As shown in the architecture diagram, the system maintains a structured flow from user interaction to data storage. Guests and staff interact with the frontend interface, which sends requests to the PHP backend. The backend processes these requests by querying the MySQL database or interacting with third-party APIs like PHPMailer or Tidio. The results are then rendered back to the user through the interface. This decoupled design allows for independent scaling of the frontend and backend components, ensuring high availability and performance even during peak resort operations.

The integration of the various modules—ranging from booking management to payroll and analytics—is managed through a secure central controller within the backend, which enforces role-based access control (RBAC). This ensures that sensitive information, such as financial data and employee records, is only accessible to authorized administrative users, while guests are restricted to their own booking and inquiry management. This architecture demonstrates how all components, users, modules, integrations, and data layers operate cohesively to support efficient resort operations.

System Test Plan
This phase outlines the development process followed for the BOSS: Web-Based Resort Management System, guided by the established design specifications and functional requirements.

Alpha Testing
The system was initially developed using two separate devices. Each side of the (customer and admin site) system's UI and functionality was coded and built independently by two of our Developers. Later on, when the UI and functionality was complete, we merged them to complete the connections on both sides. When we combined the customer and admin modules, we encountered an issue where the customer system did not reflect on the admin system. This issue could potentially risk as it could cause inaccurate monitoring on the admin side and threaten the data consistency across the system. During debugging, we discovered that the system connections weren't properly linked to the databases. Since the system was created in different devices, each system had its own databases. To fix the issue, the Developers drop the previous databases and create a new database that unites the system. We also added table prefixes to easily identify which module (customer or admin) does the table belong to each set of data.

The second testing was conducted next, to which the testing was focused on the Reservation Module, specifically on the reservation form. Upon checking if the reservation could be submitted properly, we found a problem where the system couldn't process the reservation and was showing an error message of "An error occurred while processing your reservation." The Developers tried to debug this issue and then they checked the reservation controller file, the browser interface, and the server logs in the writable/logs/ folder. There were also changes in the .env file that we looked over. After checking these directories, we found the problem was caused by the Gmail SMTP as it wasn't set up. As the Gmail SMTP wasn't set up, this also caused an issue to the email system and has caused the system to stop sending email confirmation because there is no App Password in the environment folder of the system. To fix this bug, we checked the Google Account that the system is using and then created an App Password for sending email use. Once the App Password was created, we added it on the .env file. And after applying the fix, we tried and tested the form again and then the system is now able to process the booking, send confirmation and save the data to the database.

Third testing was focused on the chatbot, we found a lot of issues that seriously affects the system and the experience of the users. One of the developers found out that the issues was in the "Waiting for an Agent" status because it is not reflecting on the admin side, a JavaScript variable also conflicts the system which stopped the real-time chat updates, the chatbot also had an infinite refresh loop after closing a case, the input field also got disabled after the agent session closure, session expiration errors caused "Case not found" messages, and also the system performed very slowly and poorly due to the N+1 queries. The fixes we implemented in these issues improved the system accessibility, performance, and overall user experience. The fixes we did were that we added the correct accessibility tags, fixed the variable conflict and improved the case closing logic to stop the refresh loop. We also reset the input field properly and optimized the database queries using the batch loading, and added automatic session recovery.

The fourth system testing was focused on the admin system. Several issues were identified specifically in the Payroll Module, Analytics Module and Role-based Module. When testing the system, we figured that these issues affected the functionality, performance and the security of the system. In the payroll module, the foreign key validation had an error when making payroll records. The validation rules that checks for the existence of staff IDs fail without warning if the staff records were deleted, especially with the inactive staff members. Another problem was that the payroll updates incorrectly automates the calculations of the gross pay, total deductions and the net pay. While in the Analytics Module, we saw a problem in the performance because of the N+1 query problems which results in longer page load times. Also the system would fail to log the errors during the data loading which caused a hardship in troubleshooting. And in the Role-Based Access Control Module, we encountered an issue that says "The action you requested is not allowed." which is a CSRF Token error, and this hindered the real user logins due to token validation failures. Also we found a security problem which could risk the hijacking because all the sessions were based on variables that could be changed, corrupted, or lost. These issues were fixed because we enhanced the payroll validation logic, and implemented the better and strong input and error handling, optimized the database queries for analytics, created a comprehensive error logging, improved the system CSRF token management, and then strengthened the role validation logics through session checks. These fixes resulted in more accurate payroll records, a lot of reduction in analytics queries count and improved the dashboard load time from several seconds to under one second, and a secured security measure which ensures the users data protection even if the data was compromised.

Before the Developers deployed the system in the web host, we did another test along with the implementation of the ISO 25010 Software Quality Standard and the security compliance testing. A lot of errors were found regarding the CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery) Token which was spread across all the systems and it blocked the users from login attempts, form submissions, and AJAX requests with the error message of "The action you requested is not allowed." This issue was because the CSRF protection was enabled globally for all the POST requests, yet the token validation is still failing because of the tokens given in request headers or the bodies did not match the cookie tokens, or the CSRF cookies weren't correctly initialized or delivered with requests. And then the Identity verification, reservation submissions, chat messaging, payroll updates, and administrative operations were disrupted. The developers solved this by implementing an integrated CSRF token management system that uses multiple fallback methods for a token retrieval, sending the tokens in request headers and bodies. The Developers also configured a proper cookie setting including the SameSite policies and then added the CSRF token meta tags to all frontend pages then implemented the automatic token refresh mechanism. These fixes enabled all the users interactions and the ISO 25010 security compliance while the CSRF protection worked properly across all the system.

Beta Testing
The Developers then created a domain and uploaded the system through a web shared hosting through Namecheap domain and their own hosting which is Stellar. When we successfully uploaded the system to the web host we still encountered multiple issues that prevented the system from running fully. These issues were mostly the repeated HTTP 503 Service Unavailable errors that appeared after putting the system files to the Namecheap shared hosting server. There were a lot of "Unavailable" problems across the system, which we detected that the cause of the issue was the incorrect setup of the database to the web host. The main problem was that the .env file didn't have the right database setting and required tables which resulted in both the site for customers and the admin system getting HTTP 503 errors.

The errors of HTTP 503 were present in the customer site when they tried to make interaction with the system such as making reservations, using the booking features like modify and transfer, and then the chatbot. This error also prevented control in the admin system which are the agent dashboard, customer service, payroll management, marketing campaign, and staff management. They couldn't view the logs, restart requests, get session information, manage escalated sessions, assign agents, and view the chat history. This error is also present in the reservation module, where booking confirmation couldn't be sent. All these errors across the system were caused by the same problem which is the database linking to the web host server as it wasn't set up correctly.

After all these fixes, which are mostly about the database connections using the right cPanel prefixed credentials, and making sure that all the necessary database tables were present, all the modules that were severely affected are fully functional now. Users can now make reservations and access the features of the system again. And in the admin side, it is now functional again, it can now access the chat logs, agent request, payroll, marketing campaigns and the analytics, they all now function as it should.

After all these changes, for the real-world testing, where the real people filled out our survey and used the system, we also tried to test the reservation being made at the same time and then we confirmed that the system handled this correctly. The first person whose screens were loaded was able to complete the booking, but the other user got an error message saying that the date is no longer available. This proved that the booking conflict logic worked correctly and that the HTTP 503 errors were gone. Finally, the system could work properly in the shared hosting and can handle multiple people at once, and get the chatbot working again, keeping the database stable during real-world usage. Implementation and Statistical Results Interpretation
Project Description
The BOSS: Blue Aura's Operational Smart System is a web-based system created to help Blue Aura manage three important tasks: booking, analytics, and payroll. Before this system was developed, most of these tasks were done manually, which often led to delays, errors, and difficulty keeping records up to date. BOSS was designed to make these processes faster, easier, and more organized by consolidating them into a single system.

The system lets the admin handle bookings, review business reports, and compute payroll without using multiple tools. It aims to make work more efficient for staff and provide a smoother experience for clients. After the development phase, the system was tested by actual users and IT experts to assess its performance in real-world scenarios.

Project Structure
The BOSS: Blue Aura's Operational Smart System for Booking, Analytics, and Payroll is structured into three major modules designed to enhance resort operations and improve user experience:

Booking Module – This module manages all reservation-related activities. It allows users to create, edit, and monitor bookings while ensuring scheduling accuracy and preventing overlapping appointments. It maintains an organized and reliable reservation process for both staff and guests.

[Figure 11: Home Page Section]

The home page serves as the main landing page for users, providing quick access to resort information, available facilities, and navigation options for booking and account management.

[Figure 12: Reservation Page]

The Reservation Page allows users to schedule their stay by selecting their preferred date, time, and services. In addition to booking, this page also serves as the account creation section, where users provide their personal information, such as name, email, and contact number. Once submitted, the system automatically stores the details and generates the user's account, enabling easier tracking and management of their reservations.

Analytics Module – Provides an overview of how the business is performing. It displays graphs, summaries, and reports based on data recorded in the system, including booking volume and activity trends. This helps the management understand what's happening in the business and make better, data-driven decisions.

The Analytics Dashboard provides a visual summary of the resort's operational performance, displaying graphs, metrics, and activity trends based on the recorded booking data. It helps the management monitor booking patterns, customer engagement, and overall system usage, enabling informed and data-driven decisions.

Payroll Module – The Payroll Module automates salary computation based on employee attendance and work hours. It automatically calculates pay, deductions, and other necessary details.

CHAPTER IV
Summary of Findings, Conclusion, and Recommendation

Summary
Based on test results and evaluations, the web-based system, "BOSS: Blue Aura's Operational Smart System for Booking, Analytics, and Payroll," was evaluated by 55 users and 7 IT experts. Based on the findings, 98% of users reported that all features worked properly, and 95% found the system easy to use. Staff and administrators were able to manage bookings, check analytics, and process payroll without confusion. Additionally, 92% of respondents said tasks were completed faster and with fewer errors than under the manual process. Guests could send queries, view their account dashboard, and pay for bookings via GCash or online banking. The system's live chat feature enabled staff to answer guest questions in real time, with 90% of users receiving prompt, helpful responses, making customer support more personal and responsive.

Evaluators gave high ratings for the system's functionality, speed, and security, with 97% expressing satisfaction in these areas. It was also found that 96% of users experienced no issues using the system across various devices, including laptops and mobile phones. Overall, the feedback indicated that the BOSS system is likely to make work easier and improve the quality of service at the resort.

Test Result

1. Functional Suitability – The test results showed that all required features of the BOSS system are present, fully operational, and performing accurately. The booking, payroll, analytics, and chatbot features work as intended with correct outputs and responses. The system's functions enable users to complete queries more quickly, resulting in booking and monitoring processes that are significantly faster than the previous manual approach.

2. Usability – The system was easy for users to understand and navigate. During testing, both staff and administrators were able to find the options they needed without any confusion. The buttons and labels were clear, and first-time users said they could use the system right away without needing much help. Overall, the system provided a smooth, comfortable experience for its users.

Evaluation Result

1. Functional Suitability – The evaluators rated the BOSS system as highly acceptable in terms of its overall functionality. They confirmed that the booking, payroll, analytics, and chatbot features are all working correctly and performing as expected. Users also noted that the system makes reservation tasks and information checks much faster than the previous manual process.

2. Performance Efficiency – The evaluators found the BOSS system's performance highly acceptable. They observed that pages load quickly, bookings are processed smoothly, and payroll calculations are completed without delay. Even when several users accessed the system simultaneously, it remained stable and responsive.

3. Usability – The evaluators rated the BOSS system as highly acceptable. They appreciated its simple and clean interface, which made it easy for both staff and administrators to navigate. They also noted that the buttons, labels, and menus were easy to understand, and that first-time users could operate the system without difficulty.

4. Security – The evaluators rated the BOSS system as highly acceptable in terms of security. They recognized that user accounts, payroll information, and booking records were protected and accessible only to authorized users. This helped build trust and confidence among staff and administrators.

5. Portability – The evaluators found the BOSS system highly acceptable in this regard. They noted that the system works smoothly across devices such as laptops, tablets, and mobile phones and remains functional across various browsers and operating systems.

Recommendations

1. To improve convenience and efficiency, it is recommended to integrate secure online payment options such as GCash, PayMaya, or debit/credit card processing. This will allow guests to complete their transactions and confirm reservations without manual follow-ups.

2. The system should automatically send confirmations, reminders, and updates on reservations, cancellations, and payment status to improve communication between the resort and its guests.

3. Since most guests use mobile devices, a fully optimized mobile interface or a dedicated mobile app is recommended to ensure smoother browsing, booking, and reservation tracking.

4. A dynamic, real-time calendar will help users easily check room availability, reduce duplicate bookings, and improve overall reservation efficiency.

5. A built-in rating or feedback system can help the management gather insights on guest satisfaction and identify areas for service improvement.

6. It is recommended that the system include automatic database backups and a recovery mechanism to prevent data loss from system failures, corruption, or other unexpected issues.

7. Regular updates, bug fixes, and usability testing should be conducted to ensure optimal performance and long-term system reliability.

Conclusion
In this project, the researchers developed the BOSS: Blue Aura's Operational Smart System for Booking, Analytics, and Payroll to support the efficient operation of Blue Aura's Hidden Paradise. By transitioning from manual procedures to a web-based platform, the resort now has a more streamlined, organized way to handle reservations, manage payroll, and monitor business performance.

The system integrates a built-in chatbot that assists users by answering common inquiries and guiding them through the platform. When the chatbot cannot address a concern, clients may connect with a live agent for further assistance. Access to the analytics dashboard is restricted to authorized staff and administrators, ensuring secure viewing of operational data and business insights. Automated processes in both booking and payroll further enhance staff efficiency, reduce the likelihood of errors, and contribute to smoother day-to-day operations.

Overall, the BOSS system provides Blue Aura with a more organized, responsive, and data-driven operational workflow. Implementing this system can significantly improve staff productivity, enhance guest support, and prepare the resort for future growth. By continuously updating and maintaining the system as the business evolves, Blue Aura can rely on BOSS to remain a valuable tool that supports both current operations and long-term development.
